Open a BO account

A Beneficiary Owner (BO) account is what lets you hold and trade shares in Bangladesh. Apply online through CDBL, or bring your documents to any ACSL branch and we will set it up with you.

What to bring

  • 2 passport-size photographs of the account holder
  • A photocopy of the account holder's NID card
  • A photocopy of the account holder's bank chequebook
  • 1 passport-size photograph of the nominee
  • A photocopy of the nominee's NID card

Apply online

Use the Central Depository Bangladesh (CDBL) online BO application and choose ACSL as your depository participant.

Thirty years on the Chattogram market

Associated Capital Securities Limited began in 1996 as a member of the Chittagong Stock Exchange, helping clients buy and sell shares listed on the port city's bourse. In 2005 the firm, its clients and its CSE membership passed to the current management team, led by Chairman MD Amin Ullah.

Under that leadership ACSL grew into one of Chattogram's leading brokerages and became one of the first in Bangladesh to offer online trading. In 2022 it added a Trading Right Entitlement Certificate on the Dhaka Stock Exchange, and today its clients trade on both exchanges through a single order management system.

The firm now serves more than ten thousand clients from four branches, offering IPO applications, secondary-market brokerage, margin loans, advisory services and internet trading.

MD Amin Ullah, Chairman. One of the leading businessmen in Bangladesh's fisheries sector, he is Managing Director of Ark Sea Foods Ltd. and president of the Bangladesh Frozen Foods Exporters Association.

Help

Which login do I use?

ACSL has two online services, each with its own login:

  • Client portal for your portfolio, ledger, buy/sell confirmations, fund deposits and withdrawals, and IPO applications.
  • ACSL Trade+ for live trading on DSE and CSE from one screen.

The same services are available in the ACSL Trade+ Android app.

I forgot my password

Use the password reset option on the login page of the client portal or the trading platform. If you cannot get back in, email it@acslbd.com or call the hotline on +880 2333 333 971 and our IT team will reset it for you.

How long does it take to open an account?

Bring the documents listed under Open a BO account to any branch and we can complete your application on the spot. Online applications through CDBL are processed once ACSL receives your details as depository participant.

Where do I download the app?

ACSL Trade+ is on Google Play. Sign in with your existing ACSL credentials.
